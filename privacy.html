Privacy

Closer doesn’t have
to mean exposed.

143 is designed so account access and linked devices stay under your control. This page is a product privacy overview and should be kept aligned with the final legal policy and backend behavior.

Account access

Your account credentials and session access should only be entered through official 143 experiences. Avoid sharing link codes or pairing details with anyone you do not trust.

Linked devices

Computers linked to your account should remain visible and revocable from the app. If you do not recognize a device, remove it and secure your account.

Transport protection

Release builds are intended to use HTTPS for network traffic. Final security claims should match the environment actually deployed and independently verified.

Screen privacy

The 143 release work includes protections intended to reduce sensitive app content appearing in system capture or app-switcher previews on supported platforms.

Honest security language

143 should not claim security properties that have not been fully implemented, tested and certified. Product copy should stay within verified behavior.
