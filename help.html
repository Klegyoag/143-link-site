Help Center

How can we help?

Quick answers for getting started, linking a computer and keeping your 143 account under control.

⌕
How do I use 143 on my computer?

Open the web login page on your computer. It starts with a QR pairing screen. Open 143 on your phone, go to Devices / Linked devices, and use the scanner when QR pairing is enabled in the app.

Why does the web page show a QR first?

The intended flow keeps the phone as the trusted primary device. The computer asks to be linked instead of making password entry the first step.

Can I remove a linked computer?

Yes. Linked-device access should be revocable from the Devices area in the mobile app.

My QR expired. What should I do?

Refresh the web login page to generate a new pairing session once live server-backed QR pairing is connected.

Can I still use the existing link-code flow?

Yes. The web login keeps the current username/password and link ID/token flow as a fallback while QR pairing is being wired to the backend.

Where can I download 143?

Open the Download page to see the Android and web options. Store links can be connected there when official distribution URLs are ready.

Need to link this computer?

Go straight to the QR-first web login.
